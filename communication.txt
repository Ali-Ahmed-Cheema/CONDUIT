IP restriction only allows calls from specific servers.

Rate limits and quotas cap how much the key can be used.
Common responses
200 means the key is valid and you got the data.
401 Unauthorized means the key is missing, invalid or expired.
403 Forbidden means the key is valid but lacks permission.
429 Too Many Requests means you hit the rate limit or quota.
How keys are sent
Header: 
Authorization: Bearer <key> or 
Query string: 
x-api-key: <key>
?key=<key> , which is less secure because it ends up in logs
Request body, which is rare
Best practices
Keep secret keys on the server in environment variables. Rotate them regularly, and
revoke any key that may have leaked.
