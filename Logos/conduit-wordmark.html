CONDUIT wordmark

One reusable component. Change --width and the whole wordmark scales, outline included.

Presets

Any size, any background

Usage

<!-- 1. once per page: the shared <svg><symbol id="conduit-wordmark">…</symbol></svg> block -->

<!-- 2. anywhere, any number of times -->
<svg class="wordmark" style="--width: 240px" viewBox="0 0 1914 335"
     role="img" aria-label="CONDUIT">
  <use href="#conduit-wordmark"/>
</svg>
